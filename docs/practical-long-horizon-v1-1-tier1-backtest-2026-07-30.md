# Practical Long Horizon v1.1 Tier-1 Backtest

## Scope

The practical backtest applies the frozen Long Horizon v1.1 target contracts
to hash-verified controlled inputs and completed-session outcomes. Business
quality and security attractiveness remain separate; expected return and
downside risk abstain when their required evidence is incomplete.

This route is bounded retrospective development evidence, not an untouched
holdout, strict PIT proof, or promise of future returns.
