# 2026-07-26: Analysis Product Design

## Product Clarification

- Defined stock assessment as an input to portfolio decisions rather than the
  final product output.
- Separated company quality, price attractiveness, near-term market condition,
  evidence coverage, and portfolio fit.
- Confirmed that a good company may be unsuitable at its current valuation or
  for a user's existing portfolio.
- Defined the future user context as investment goals, risk profile, sector
  preferences, cash, liabilities, leverage constraints, holdings, and allowed
  rebalancing scope.

## Investment Approach

- Adopted a Graham-inspired long-term discipline centered on financial
  strength, business quality, valuation, margin of safety, diversification, and
  human judgment.
- Distinguished defensive investing, enterprising investing, and explicitly
  limited speculation from investment horizon.
- Deferred a generic medium-term score until it has a distinct use case and
  testable methodology.
- Kept near-term market condition separate from long-term investment
  assessment.

## Screening Architecture

- Defined a two-stage research funnel:
  - Broad, deterministic quantitative screening
  - Source-backed AI evidence review for prioritized candidates
- Defined initial strategy paths as `Quality Compounder` and
  `Undervalued Quality`.
- Deferred `Emerging Growth` and specialized bank, insurer, REIT, resource,
  biotechnology, and special-situation models.
- Required comparison within sector, size, company-type, and strategy cohorts
  instead of a universal cross-company score.
- Required explicit coverage states so quantitatively scored, AI-reviewed,
  stale, insufficient-data, and specialized-model cases cannot be confused.

## Sector Design

- Separated sector classification from sector scoring.
- Defined an initial ETF-based sector market-condition assessment relative to
  `SPY`.
- Deferred sector fundamental attractiveness until a broad point-in-time
  constituent dataset is available.
- Prohibited use of the current six-security engineering set as evidence for a
  sector rating.

## AI Role

- Kept deterministic code responsible for calculations, eligibility, ranking,
  constraints, and backtesting.
- Assigned AI to structured evidence extraction, contradiction discovery,
  qualitative risk review, and cited explanation.
- Rejected unrestricted AI-generated numeric scores.
- Prioritized AI review for user holdings, explicit requests, watchlists,
  high-ranking candidates, material changes, and new filings.

## Data Strategy

- Confirmed that the existing Twelve Data integration remains useful for the
  current engineering slice and as a replaceable price provider.
- Selected SEC EDGAR as the preferred primary-source filing and XBRL evidence
  provider.
- Identified EODHD as the leading paid candidate for a one-month personal
  research validation, subject to field, point-in-time, delisted-data, and
  licensing acceptance tests.
- Deferred annual subscriptions and a final vendor commitment.
- Required a 20-security validation set before a 300-to-500-security
  stratified sample and broader United States coverage.

## Competitive Positioning

- Recognized that quantitative ratings, AI stock reports, and portfolio health
  tools already have mature competitors.
- Reframed the potential differentiator as a portfolio-aware, evidence-based
  decision and review workflow rather than another AI stock score.
- Identified the proposed distinguishing workflow as candidate discovery,
  adversarial evidence review, new-money and rebalancing comparisons, human
  decisions, and longitudinal thesis evaluation.
- Kept these advantages as hypotheses until demonstrated through working
  software and user validation.
