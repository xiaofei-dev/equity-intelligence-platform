# Tactical v2.2 Tier-1 Statistical Closeout

## Decision

The statistical closeout applies the frozen dependency-aware resampling and
risk-measurement contract to the controlled Tactical v2.2 retrospective.

Its numeric estimates and intervals are derived from personal-use or licensed
market data. The report is therefore a local controlled artifact and is not
published in Git.

The public claim remains limited to these facts:

- the model and cost policies are versioned and reproducible;
- the run is retrospective `DEVELOPMENT_OBSERVED` evidence;
- no untouched-holdout, strict PIT, or forward-validation claim is made;
- no AI ranking, automatic trading, or formula retuning is authorized;
- missing evidence remains explicit.

See [Licensed Market Data Publication Policy](licensed-market-data-publication-policy.md).
